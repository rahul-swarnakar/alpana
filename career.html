Careers & Opportunities at ALPANA

Join our dedicated team working at the grassroots level in Birbhum district to strengthen rural livelihoods, promote awareness, and empower disadvantaged communities.

Why ALPANA

Making a real difference where it matters most

Working at ALPANA offers deep community immersion, hands-on development experience, and the satisfaction of building sustainable rural futures.

01

Grassroots Immersion

Work directly alongside artisans, farmers, self-help groups, and village institutions across Bolpur and surrounding blocks.

02

Collaborative Growth

Be part of an open, supportive, and participatory team culture where initiative and community empathy are highly valued.

03

Meaningful Impact

Contribute to transparent, need-based interventions that tangibly improve family incomes and community resilience.
