What we do

Three threads, one pattern

ALPANA's name is also its method — each letter names a thread of the same fabric.

01

Livelihood Promotion

Skill training in terracotta craft, handloom weaving, bamboo & cane work, and support to reach fair markets for what artisans already know how to make.

02

Awareness

Village-level meetings, wall art and street theatre on health, education, entitlements and safe practice — carried by people the community already trusts.

03

Need Assessment

Door-to-door survey and household mapping before any programme begins, so what we build answers what a village has actually asked for.

In the field

Birbhum's crafts, given a fair chance

Bolpur and its surrounding villages carry generations of skill — Kantha embroidery, terracotta idols and diyas, cane and bamboo basketry, Santiniketan leatherwork. ALPANA's livelihood work starts by recognising that skill, then closing the gap between a well-made object and a fair buyer.

Why an alpona

A pattern drawn with the community, not for it

An alpona is never stencilled — it is drawn afresh each time, by hand, to fit the doorstep it sits on. That is how we try to work: no two villages get the same plan; each one gets a pattern fitted to what's already there.
