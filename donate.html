Support Rural Empowerment in Birbhum

Your support directly empowers rural artisans, traditional craftswomen, and vulnerable families across Birbhum district through sustainable livelihoods and grassroots development.

Your Impact

How Your Contribution Creates Real Change

Every contribution directly strengthens community-led programs on the ground in Bolpur and surrounding villages.

01

Artisan Skill Toolkits

Provides raw materials, quality tools, and market linkages for terracotta artisans, Kantha embroiderers, and bamboo craftworkers.

02

Community Awareness

Supports village-level workshops on health, women's rights, child education, and government entitlement access.

03

Grassroots Need Mapping

Funds door-to-door household surveys to ensure development programs solve actual village priorities.
