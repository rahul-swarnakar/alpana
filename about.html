Who we are

About ALPANA

Association for Livelihood Promotion, Awareness and Need Assessment (ALPANA) is a community-based, non-profit organisation working for the socio-economic development and empowerment of rural and disadvantaged communities in Birbhum district of West Bengal. ALPANA is a registered society established in 2013 and is based in Bolpur, Birbhum.

The organisation works with a community-centred approach, focusing on understanding local needs and developing programmes according to the priorities, skills and resources available within the community. ALPANA aims to strengthen sustainable livelihoods, improve awareness, promote skill development and facilitate better access to opportunities and essential services.

Guiding Principles

Vision & Mission

Vision

To contribute towards an inclusive and self-reliant society where rural and disadvantaged communities have access to sustainable livelihoods, knowledge, skills, opportunities and essential services.

Mission

To empower communities through need-based livelihood promotion, skill development, awareness generation, participatory planning and community-based development initiatives, while strengthening local resources, traditional skills and community institutions.

Our Focus

Key Areas of Work

01

1. Livelihood Promotion

ALPANA promotes sustainable livelihood opportunities among rural communities, with particular focus on traditional artisans and local producers. The organisation supports skill development and capacity building in areas such as terracotta, handloom and weaving, bamboo and cane crafts, Kantha embroidery and other traditional livelihood activities. It also works towards improving market linkages and promoting fair opportunities for local producers and artisans.

02

2. Awareness and Community Development

ALPANA conducts community-level awareness activities on important social and developmental issues, including health, education, government schemes and entitlements, livelihood opportunities and safe practices. The organisation uses participatory approaches such as village meetings, awareness campaigns, wall art and street-based communication to encourage community participation.

03

3. Need Assessment and Community Planning

ALPANA follows a need-based approach to programme development. Household-level surveys, community consultations and local-level assessments are undertaken to understand the socio-economic conditions, needs and priorities of communities before designing interventions. This helps the organisation develop programmes that are relevant to local requirements.

Our roots

Why "Alpona"

An alpona is the traditional Bengali floor art drawn in rice paste at the threshold of a home — for a festival, a wedding, a harvest. It is never copied wholesale from one doorstep to the next; it is drawn fresh, by hand, to suit the space in front of it.

We took the name because it describes how we try to work: livelihood promotion, awareness, and need assessment are not three separate departments, but three strokes of the same pattern, drawn together, household by household.
