Programmes

Three strokes of the same pattern

Livelihood promotion, awareness and need assessment run alongside each other — each shapes the other two.

01 — Livelihood Promotion

Building on the crafts Bolpur already knows

Birbhum's villages carry generations of skill in clay, thread, and cane. Our livelihood work trains, organises and connects that skill to fairer markets.

Terracotta & clay craft

Skill-upgrade workshops for potters and idol-makers, and help preparing pieces — diyas, figures, garden ware — for festival and retail markets.

Handloom & Kantha embroidery

Training and design support for weavers and Kantha stitch artisans, with an eye on the same running-stitch patience an alpona also asks for.

Bamboo & cane basketry

Support for basket, mat and homeware weavers to standardise sizes and reach buyers beyond the local haat.

02 — Awareness

Carried by people the village already trusts

We run village meetings, wall-writing campaigns and small group sessions on health, sanitation, girls' education and government entitlements — led wherever possible by local volunteers rather than outside staff.

  • Household and school-level health & hygiene sessions
  • Information on entitlements and welfare schemes
  • Support for girls' school retention and safety
03 — Need Assessment

Before we plan, we ask

Every intervention starts with a door-to-door household survey — who is farming what, which children are in school, what skills are already in the house, and what a family says it actually needs.

  • Household and village-level baseline surveys
  • Mapping of existing skills, land and resources
  • Findings shared back with the village before any programme starts
